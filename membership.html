Membership

One year at a time.

Nualia is a membership rather than a scan, for one reason: a single image is a photograph, and the thing worth having is the sequence.

What is included

The annual scan

Whole-body MRI, around forty-five minutes. No radiation, no needles, no contrast.

A signed report

Read with licensed software, signed by a consultant radiologist, written to be read by you rather than by another doctor.

The consultation

A doctor takes you through the findings in plain language, in person, with time to ask.

The comparison

From your second year onward, every scan is read against your own previous scans.

Your record, held for you

Images and reports kept securely under UAE data rules, and yours to take with you.

Referral, if needed

A route to the right specialist, with no fee to us from anyone we refer to.

Membership is annual, and includes everything above. We will publish what it costs once it is settled.

Year one, and after

The second year is the valuable one.

In year one we build your baseline and tell you what we can see. In year two the question changes entirely. No longer does this look normal, but has this changed. That is a far better question, and only a returning member can ask it.

It is also why we do not sell a one-off scan as though it were the product. You are welcome to come once. We would simply rather be honest that you would be buying the least useful version of this.

For companies

Executive screening, medically governed.

Corporate executive health budgets in this region routinely buy check-ups that never look inside the head, the pancreas or the ovaries. We offer the imaging half, with the governance written down: signed reads, an explicit referral boundary, and a consent conversation about incidental findings before anyone is scanned.

Employers receive no clinical information about any individual. Ever. That is not a policy we would negotiate.
