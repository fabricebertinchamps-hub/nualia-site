Privacy

Privacy and your data.

What this page is

A plain-language summary of how Nualia handles information. It is written to be read, not to be survived. A full policy will sit alongside it before the service opens.

Your images and reports are yours

Scans and reports are held securely, within the United Arab Emirates, under UAE health-data rules. You can request a copy at any time, in a standard format, and take it to any other clinician.

What we collect from this website

In the live site, only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, your email, a preferred time, and anything optional you choose to add. Nothing else.

Please do not send us medical details by email or chat

We cannot give medical advice that way and we would rather not hold clinical information in an inbox. Anything clinical belongs in a consultation.

No tracking cookies

We use no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking. That is also why this site has no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.

Employers never receive clinical information

Where a company pays for memberships, the employer is told who attended and nothing more. Never a finding, never a report.

Who to contact

privacy@nualia.health reaches the person responsible for data protection at Nualia.

This summary is not legal advice and is not the full policy.
